# BFCM Campaign-in-a-Box — Sample Pack

**Store:** Northline Coffee Co. (illustrative DTC roaster — used to show the method, not a client)
**Offer:** 20% off sitewide · free shipping over $60 · two gift bundles
**Window:** Tue Nov 24, 8:00pm ET → Mon Nov 30, 11:59pm ET, 2026
**Status: DRAFTS ONLY.** Nothing here is scheduled, sent, or connected to a live account.

**Audience:** 12,400 subscribers, 61% open rate on the last 90 days, average order $52.
**Voice read from the store:** warm, dry, specific. Short sentences. No exclamation marks. Product names in title case. The owner writes "we", never "Northline is pleased to".

## Email 1 — Early access opens tonight

**Send:** Tue Nov 24, 8:00pm ET · full list (exclude buyers from the last 7 days)
**Job:** Reward the list with first pick and set the three facts of the offer.

**Subject:** Early access starts at 8
**Alt A:** You get first pick, starting tonight
**Alt B:** Tomorrow this goes public
**Preview:** 20% off, free shipping over $60, and two bundles. No code needed.

**Body**

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Every year we open Black Friday to the people who actually drink our coffee before we open it to the internet. That's you. Tonight at 8pm ET the whole store goes 20% off, and shipping is free over $60.
>
> Three things worth knowing before you look:
>
> **The Morning Set.** House Blend, Night Shift Decaf, and the Ethiopia Guji, one bag each. $54 instead of $69.
>
> **The Gift Box.** Two bags, our stoneware mug, and a card we write by hand. $62 instead of $78.
>
> **The 2lb bags.** Same coffee, better per-cup price, and they're included in the 20%.
>
> No code needed. Prices change at 8pm and change back Monday at midnight.
>
> [ Shop early access ]
>
> — Dana
> Northline Coffee Co.
>
> P.S. The Gift Box sold out on day three last year. If you're buying for someone, don't wait for the weekend.

**Why this works:** Subscribers get a reason to be subscribers, and the offer is fully explained in the first send so later emails can be short. "No code needed" removes the most common Black Friday friction: a lost code.

## Email 2 — The bundle math

**Send:** Fri Nov 27, 6:00am ET · non-openers of Email 1
**Job:** Convert the people who ignored a warm-up email with arithmetic instead of urgency.

**Subject:** Three bags for $18 each
**Alt A:** The math on the Morning Set
**Alt B:** Black Friday, by the numbers
**Preview:** $54 for three bags. Here's what that is per cup.

**Body**

> Hi {{first_name}},
>

> The Morning Set is $54 for three 12oz bags. That's **$18 a bag**, and about **31 cents a cup** at our usual dose.
>
> Two bags on their own, with 20% off and free shipping over $60, comes to $35.20. The third bag costs you $18.80 more and gets you through December.
>
> The 2lb Big Bag is $36.80 today. Same coffee, roughly 90 cups, 41 cents a cup if you brew it strong.
>
> Everything ships free over $60. Nothing needs a code.
>
> [ Shop Black Friday ]
>
> — Dana
>
> P.S. If you already know what you want, the Gift Box is the one that runs out. Two bags, a mug, a handwritten card.

**Why this works:** Price-per-cup is the unit this audience already thinks in. Arithmetic is more persuasive than "our biggest sale of the year", and it survives being read on a phone at 6am.

## Abandoned checkout — three tweaks to the flow you already have

These are edits to the Shopify/Klaviyo flow Northline already runs. No new tooling.

### Tweak 1 — Move the first email from 4 hours to 1 hour, and remove the discount

**Now:** 4h, "You left something behind", 10% code.
**Change to:** 1h, product-specific subject, no code.

> **Subject:** Still thinking about the {{product_name}}?
> **Preview:** It's in your cart. Shipping is free over $60.
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> The {{product_name}} is still in your cart. We saved it.
>
> If you were deciding between that and the {{related_product}}, both are 20% off until Monday. Shipping is free over $60.
>
> [ Finish checkout ]
>
> — Dana

**Why:** The 1-hour window catches people who were interrupted, not people who were hesitating. Discounting at 1 hour teaches your list to abandon on purpose.

### Tweak 2 — Put the discount at 48 hours, capped to carts over $60

**Now:** 10% at 24h, sitewide.
**Change to:** 15% at 48h, only where cart value is over $60, with a single-use code.

> **Subject:** 15% off the cart you left
> **Preview:** Code NORTHLINE15. It works until midnight tomorrow.
>
> You left {{cart_value}} of coffee in your cart. Here's 15% to finish it: **NORTHLINE15**.
>
> It works once, until midnight tomorrow, on carts over $60. After that we'll stop emailing you about it.

**Why:** The discount now has a condition and a deadline, so it reads as a decision rather than a standing policy. Capping it to $60+ protects margin on small carts.

### Tweak 3 — Add one SMS at 2 hours, only for carts over $75

**Send:** 2h after abandonment · carts over $75 · SMS opt-ins only

> Northline: your cart is still saved. Free shipping over $60 and 20% off ends Monday. {{link}}
> Reply STOP to opt out.

**Why:** High-value carts deserve a second channel. Sending it to every cart is how a number gets burned.

## Setup checklist (buyer-facing, included in every pack)

1. Import the five emails into Klaviyo as a **campaign draft**, not a flow. Do not schedule.
2. Replace `{{first_name}}`, `{{product_name}}`, `{{related_product}}`, `{{cart_value}}`, and `{{link}}` with your store's merge tags. Test with a real profile.
3. Build the three segments: full list, non-openers (no open in 7 days), purchasers suppressed.
4. Set the SMS sends only to people with explicit SMS consent. Import the opt-in list, do not reuse the email list.
5. Send one test of each email to yourself on a phone. Check the button, the images, and the P.S.
6. Confirm the discount is applied at the product level, not with a code, so the sitewide sale and the emails agree.
7. Schedule the five emails and three texts against the calendar above, then stop.

## What changes for your store

This pack was written from Northline's catalogue: 12 products, three price tiers, a subscription, and a gift bundle. Yours gets the same structure with your products, your prices, your shipping threshold, and your voice. The send calendar moves to your sale dates. Nothing else in the method changes.

Built in one pass from the store URL, then reviewed against the store's own product pages for names, prices, and shipping terms.
